Where do tech workers live in Reno?

Three patterns dominate: remote and hybrid workers cluster where lifestyle is — Midtown's walkability, Somersett's trails, the view corridors; Tahoe-Reno Industrial Center (TRIC) commuters solve for the eastern drive — Spanish Springs, Sparks, and Dayton make that math work; and families optimizing space and newer builds pick the master-planned south and north valleys. NV Ball Real Estate Group maps your actual commute, team cadence, and life — not a stereotype — onto the neighborhoods that fit.

Straight answers

Where do tech workers live in Reno?

Three clusters: remote workers buy lifestyle (Midtown, Somersett, the view corridors); TRIC commuters buy the eastern math (Spanish Springs, Sparks, Dayton); families buy master-planned space (Damonte, South Reno). Your commute and life decide — not the stereotype.

How far is the commute to TRIC/the Gigafactory?

Spanish Springs/east Sparks: commonly ~half an hour down USA Parkway. Dayton: similar by the country route. South Reno: stretches. We quote your shift-hour reality, not map optimism.

Is Reno's tech scene big enough to change jobs within?

Growing and real — TRIC's corridor plus a seeded startup scene — but not Bay-liquidity, and honesty matters: remote-friendly careers travel best; single-employer moves deserve a plan B.
