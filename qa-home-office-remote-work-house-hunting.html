For remote workers · The office is the house now

House hunting for remote work: the checklist that actually matters

Remote workers fund a big share of Northern Nevada's migration, and the houses that serve them share verifiable features — not vibes: a real office (door that closes, not a loft landing; video-call background and light; distance from the kitchen's gravity), internet verified at the address before the offer (provider claims die at specific houses — we test, not trust), power resilience where outages visit (mountain and rural addresses especially), and the zoning-adjacent questions if clients ever visit. NV Ball Real Estate Group buys for remote workers weekly — here's the checklist experience built.

Run the WFH checklist

The office that functions

A door that closes beats square footage: call privacy, video background, natural light that doesn't backlight you at 2pm, and physical distance from household gravity (kitchens, laundry, the TV). Two-remote-worker households need the second space planned, not improvised — the pandemic taught everyone which homes failed.

Internet, verified not trusted

Provider coverage maps lie at the address level: we verify actual service (fiber pockets exist and matter; cable tiers vary block by block; rural means the rural-internet picture) during diligence, with speeds in writing where careers depend on them. The question costs nothing before an offer and everything after.

The resilience layer

Mountain and rural addresses meet outage reality (the PSOM-and-generators brief): backup power thinking belongs in the purchase where your income streams through the wall. City addresses mostly shrug; your job's tolerance decides the weight.

Straight answers

What should remote workers look for when buying a house?

The verifiable four: a real office (door, light, background, distance), address-verified internet (maps lie; we test), power resilience where relevant, and business-use questions if clients visit. Experience built the list.

How do I verify internet before buying a house?

Address-specific, in diligence: exact-address lookups, confirmation calls, seller speed documentation, and the rural honest picture. Standard practice here — careers deserve better than coverage-map optimism.

Do home offices add resale value in Reno?

For the buyer class funding this migration — increasingly yes: genuine offices and verified internet show and sell better. Purpose-built beats improvised, both directions.
