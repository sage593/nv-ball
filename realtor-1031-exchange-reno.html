Straight answers

How does a 1031 exchange work in Reno?

Sell an investment property, buy like-kind replacement through a qualified intermediary, defer the gain — with two unforgiving clocks: 45 days to identify, 180 to close. We coordinate both legs so the deadlines work for you, not against you.

What counts as like-kind property?

Investment real property for investment real property — rental for duplex, land for commercial. Your own home doesn't qualify. Edge cases go to your intermediary and CPA; we execute to their ruling.

Why do 1031 exchanges fail, and how do you prevent it?

Timing kills exchanges — closing the sale with no realistic replacements identified, or financing that drags past day 180. Our fix: replacement candidates lined up before your sale closes, and offer terms written to the exchange calendar.
