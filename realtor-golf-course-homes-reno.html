Fairway living · The regional circuit

Golf course homes in Reno, NV — the full map

Northern Nevada's fairway circuit runs from benchmark to bargain: Montrêux's Nicklaus Signature and ArrowCreek's two courses in the foothills, Somersett and Lakeridge west, Hidden Valley's classic club east, Red Hawk in Sparks, Dayton Valley's value fairways, and Genoa Lakes under the Carson Valley's peaks. Each community prices by its own logic, separates residency from membership differently, and deals fairway lots their own ball-exposure realities. We work the whole circuit — which is what lets us match the golfer to the right one.

Straight answers

Where are the golf course homes in Reno and Northern Nevada?

The circuit runs: Montrêux and ArrowCreek in the foothills, Somersett and Lakeridge west, Hidden Valley's classic east-side club, Red Hawk in Sparks, Dayton Valley's value fairways, Genoa Lakes south. Different courses, different price logic — we play matchmaker.

What should fairway-home buyers check?

Ball exposure by hole position, the HOA rulebook versus the club's separate membership terms, and the course operator's health. A fairway home is a bet on all three — we check them.

Do you have to join the club to live there?

Usually no — living there and joining are separate decisions in most of our communities. Terms differ and evolve; we brief you current before any offer.
