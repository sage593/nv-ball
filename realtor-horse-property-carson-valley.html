Straight answers

Who sells horse property in the Carson Valley?

We do — across the Carson Valley, Spanish Springs, and rural Washoe. More importantly, we handle what these sales actually turn on: wells, septic, fencing, outbuildings, and the disclosures city agents miss.

What should I check before buying horse property in Nevada?

Well capacity, water rights, septic, fencing, zoning and animal counts, winter access, open-range rules. The unglamorous list is the whole ballgame — we walk every parcel with it.

Do horse properties sell differently?

Yes — specific buyers, often from out of area, and the arena matters as much as the kitchen. Our listings shoot and market the facilities properly.
