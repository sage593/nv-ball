Deciding · The regional fork

Should I live in Reno or Lake Tahoe?

The fork, honestly drawn: Tahoe means living inside the postcard — alpine mornings, lake light, and the full winter commitment, priced by scarcity and served by village-scale amenities. Reno means livability — airport, hospitals, housing variety, milder winters — with the postcard a 45-minute drive you'll actually make. Our years of running both markets produce one reliable observation: most households want Tahoe access more than Tahoe addresses, and the minority who truly want the lake life recognize themselves immediately. Both answers are right; mixing them up is the only mistake.
