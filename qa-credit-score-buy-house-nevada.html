Buying · Qualification

What credit score do I need to buy a house in Nevada?

The floors, plainly: conventional loans generally start around 620, FHA reaches as low as 500 (with larger down payments below 580), VA runs about 620 by lender policy, USDA about 640. Two truths travel with those numbers: minimums get you considered while stronger scores price better rates — and lower-credit buyers are consistently closer to qualifying than they assume. The free lender conversation surprises people weekly; it's the highest-value fifteen minutes in this business.

Straight answers

What credit score do I need to buy a house in Nevada?

Common floors: conventional ~620, FHA as low as 500 (bigger down below 580), VA ~620 by lender policy, USDA ~640. Floors get you in the door; stronger scores buy better rates. Lower-credit buyers are usually closer than they think — the lender chat is free.

My score is below those numbers — now what?

A path, not a wall: lender-guided credit roadmaps move scores faster than people expect, and program-switching sometimes qualifies you now. Don't rent another year on an assumption.

Modestly and briefly — and shopping within the window counts once. The information outvalues the points, every time.
