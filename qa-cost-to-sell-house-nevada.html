Selling · Money

How much does it cost to sell a house in Nevada?

Five line items do most of the work: agent compensation (negotiable, agreed in your listing contract), Nevada's transfer tax (customarily seller-paid in many counties), your share of title and escrow fees, any credits negotiated during escrow, and the preparation you choose to invest up front. Setting aside your mortgage payoff, these typically total mid-to-high single digits as a percentage of price — and a written net sheet, prepared before you list, turns that range into your actual number.

Straight answers

How much does it cost to sell a house in Nevada?

The lines that matter: agent compensation (negotiable, set in your listing agreement), transfer tax (customarily seller-paid in many counties), your share of title/escrow, negotiated credits, and chosen prep. We prepare a written net sheet before you list — real numbers, not ranges.

Is the commission negotiable in Nevada?

Yes — by law it's negotiable, set by agreement. The real question is what you get for it, and our launch plan is happy to be compared line by line.

What's a seller net sheet?

Your bottom line in writing — price minus payoff, costs, taxes, credits — prepared before listing and updated with every offer. Decisions on numbers, not vibes.
