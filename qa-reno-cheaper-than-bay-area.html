Relocation · Cost of living

Is it cheaper to live in Reno than the Bay Area?

For the two biggest line items — housing and state income tax — generally yes: comparable homes typically cost meaningfully less than in most Bay Area metros, and Nevada taxes none of your income at the state level. The honest fine print: Reno has grown out of bargain territory, a few costs run higher, and the real answer depends on which city you're leaving and which neighborhood you're choosing.

Straight answers

Is it cheaper to live in Reno than the Bay Area?

For housing and state income tax, generally yes. But Reno isn't the bargain it was a decade ago, and the real comparison depends on which Bay Area metro you're leaving and which Reno neighborhood you're targeting. We'll run your actual numbers rather than an average.
