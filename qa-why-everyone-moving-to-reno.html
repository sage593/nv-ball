The region · The big picture

Why is everyone moving to Reno?

Four engines, running at once: Nevada's no-income-tax pull on California households; thousands of jobs at the industrial center east of Sparks; remote work untethering Bay Area earners who always wanted the mountains; and lifestyle math — Tahoe at 45 minutes, four genuine seasons, an international airport — that makes the move feel like an upgrade instead of an exile. The growth has costs (traffic, prices, nostalgia) and the honest summary is simple: more city, more cost, same mountains.

Straight answers

Why is everyone moving to Reno?

Is the growth changing Reno?

Visibly — new rooftops, a better food scene, more traffic, higher prices. More city, more cost, same mountains. Most transplants still take the trade.

Will the growth continue?

We don't do crystal balls. Observable: the tax advantage, job base, and geography are structural — and they're the engines. Draw your own curve.
