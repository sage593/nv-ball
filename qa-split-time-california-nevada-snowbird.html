Relocation · The both-places plan

Splitting time between California and Nevada: how to do it right

Plenty of households live the split: a Nevada home for most of the year, California ties that persist — or the reverse, a Tahoe or Reno second home against a California base. The real estate part is straightforward and we run it daily. The tax part is where deliberateness matters: which state is your domicile is a facts-and-circumstances question with real money attached, and California examines it seriously. We handle the homes; a CPA draws your residency line — and the plan works best decided before the purchase, not after.

Plan the two-home life

The second-home purchase

Financing, insurance, and care differ for a part-time home — especially at Tahoe (snow, caretaking) and in HOA communities with rental rules. We buy these with the absentee months designed in.

The residency line

Domicile is facts and circumstances — where you're registered, doctored, days counted — and California's scrutiny of departing or part-time residents is famously real. Our residency guide maps the steps; your CPA owns the line.

The eventual consolidation

Many split-staters eventually choose one address — and the sequencing of which home sells when has capital-gains consequences worth planning years ahead. We keep the long file.

Straight answers

Can I split time between California and Nevada?

Yes, and many do. Homes: routine — we handle that. Domicile: the deliberate part — days, registrations, where life centers — with real money attached and real California scrutiny. CPA draws the line, ideally before the purchase.

Does buying a Nevada home make me a Nevada resident?

No. A deed isn't a domicile — residency follows your life's whole pattern, and the house alone proving it is the classic expensive assumption. Necessary piece, not sufficient.

What makes a good split-time home at Tahoe or in Reno?

Lock-and-leave resilience: someone who notices, systems that tolerate absence, snow/defensible-space plans at elevation, rental rules pre-checked. We shop these with the empty months designed in.
