What living here is like

Lakeridge is west Reno's old money in cardigans: an enclave that matured around its golf course decades ago — the island-green par-3 is the region's most photographed hole — with custom homes, fairway frontage, and townhome tiers sharing the same settled address. Everything the west side considers essential sits within minutes, downtown included, and the neighborhood's calm says prestige without raising its voice.

Who Lakeridge fits

Strong fit: established-prestige buyers who prefer settled over shiny; golfers drawn to the classic course and its postcard hole; west-side loyalists wanting ten-minute-everything; downsizers eyeing the townhome and condo tiers inside the same address.

Honest considerations: 1970s–90s construction invites the full systems inspection; enclave inventory is scarce and turnover slow; fairway and water frontage carry premiums needing true comps; and HOA/condo tiers each read by their own documents — association-audit rules apply.

Lakeridge FAQ

Is Lakeridge a good place to live?

Yes — it's the west side's quiet classic: storied course, settled customs, ten-minute everything. Inspect like it's 1985 (because the pipes might be) and price frontage on true comps.

What's the famous hole?

The island-green par-3 — played nervously, photographed relentlessly. Sightline homes price accordingly, and honestly, fair enough.

Lakeridge or Caughlin Ranch?

Club-classic versus trail-green: identity at Lakeridge, structure at Caughlin. The west side wins either way.
