Relocation · The practical checklist

Setting up utilities when you move to Reno

The short version: NV Energy handles electricity (and gas in most of the area), TMWA — the Truckee Meadows Water Authority — supplies water across Reno-Sparks, Waste Management covers most trash service, and internet runs through a handful of providers whose availability is address-specific. Rural and outlying addresses swap in wells, septic, and propane. NV Ball Real Estate Group hands every buyer a setup sheet for their exact address at closing — here's the general map.

Get my address's checklist

The core four

Power and gas: NV Energy for most addresses. Water: TMWA in Reno-Sparks (Carson City and the valleys have their own utilities). Trash: Waste Management predominately. Internet: address-specific — we check your exact options during escrow, not after.

The rural swap

Acreage and outlying homes trade utilities for systems you own: well, septic, propane. Those get inspected in escrow and budgeted for — the rural-diligence guide runs deeper.

The timing that works

Start transfers about two weeks out; power and water same-day as closing, internet installs booked early (slots fill). Our closing sheet sequences it so move-in day is boring — the goal.

Straight answers

Who provides utilities in Reno?

Power/gas: NV Energy. Water: TMWA in Reno-Sparks. Trash: mostly Waste Management. Internet: depends on the address. Outside town, wells, septic, and propane take over — different checklist, same planning.

When should I set up utilities before moving?

Two weeks out: power/water effective closing day, internet booked early (slots fill), trash cart confirmed. Start at closing instead, and you'll live politely by flashlight for a bit.

Do you help with this?

Yes — every closing comes with an address-specific setup sheet: providers, contacts, calling order. Unglamorous, and exactly what a team should just handle.
