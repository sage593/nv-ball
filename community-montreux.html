What living here is like

Montrêux is where Reno's luxury market measures itself: guard-gated pines, a Nicklaus Signature course that hosted PGA Tour golf for two decades, and custom homes built to belong to the forest around them. The elevation buys cool summers, real winters, and a fifteen-minute run to Mt. Rose's lifts; the gate buys the quiet. This is flagship territory for our luxury division, and the pricing conversation here deserves specialists.

Who Montrêux fits

Strong fit: luxury buyers who want the region's benchmark address; golfers (obviously — the course is the region's standard); skiers who count minutes to Mt. Rose; privacy-first households for whom the gate is the point.

Honest considerations: elevation means genuine winter — snow management is a household system here; club membership economics deserve understanding before you buy; the drive to town is beautiful and unhurried, which is also to say twenty-plus minutes; and a thin, specific market demands true comparables — our division's daily work.

Montrêux FAQ

Is Montrêux the best golf community in Reno?

It's the benchmark the others are measured against — Nicklaus Signature golf, two decades of PGA Tour history, and the custom standard. Whether it's 'best for you' is the conversation we'd love to have.

How is winter at Montrêux?

Real winter — heated driveways and plowing contracts are standard equipment. The same elevation hands you Mt. Rose's lifts in fifteen minutes.

Do I need to join the club?

Membership economics are part of the purchase math, and terms evolve — we brief buyers on the current structure before any offer.
