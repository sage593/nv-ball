Relocation · The recon weekend

The Reno scouting trip: 48 hours that de-risk the move

The move-deciding weekend has a method: day one runs the shortlist neighborhoods at honest hours (morning commute windows, evening walk-arounds — the three-question sort done beforehand turns forty areas into four), plus the daily-life reality checks (your actual grocery run, the gym, the drive to whatever your Saturday needs); day two climbs — Tahoe via Mt. Rose Highway (the lifestyle argument makes itself) — and tests the finalists at different hours. The rules that upgrade it: the two-season principle (whatever season you visit, plan the second trip in its opposite — or at minimum tour your finalist in its hardest months), skip the casino-corridor hotel trap (stay in Midtown-adjacent lodging and meet the real city), and end with the conversation that converts recon into plan. NV Ball Real Estate Group builds these itineraries weekly — here's the template.

Build my recon weekend

Day one: the shortlist at honest hours

Morning: drive your real commute at its real time. Midday: the finalist neighborhoods walked (groceries, coffee, the park — daily life's actual geography). Evening: the same blocks after dark (the diligence hour nobody schedules). The pre-trip sort matters: arrive with four candidates, not forty — the capstone brief preps the cut.

Day two: the argument and the verification

Morning up Mt. Rose Highway — Tahoe makes the region's case in ninety minutes better than any page — then back for finalist second-passes at different hours, the school tours where families scheduled them, and the open houses that calibrate your budget against walls you've touched.

The rules that upgrade it

Two seasons (visit winter if you came in summer — or at minimum tour the finalist in its hardest months before closing), real-city lodging (Midtown-adjacent beats the casino corridor for meeting the town you'd actually live in), and the exit conversation — an hour with us converts the weekend's impressions into a sequenced plan: rent-first or buy, timeline, and the next trip's purpose.

Straight answers

How should I plan a visit to Reno before moving there?

48 hours, method: pre-sorted shortlist, day one at honest hours (real commute, walks at noon AND after dark, your actual errands), day two up to Tahoe + finalist second-passes. Upgrades: two seasons, real-city lodging, the exit conversation.

What's the biggest scouting-trip mistake?

The resort-weekend version: corridor hotel, Tahoe-only days, windshield-at-noon neighborhoods — verifies nothing. Honest hours fix it: real commutes, after-dark blocks, actual errands.

Should I look at houses on a scouting trip?

For calibration: open houses teach the budget's truth fast. Commitment waits for the settled sort — usually. Pressure-free touring is the productive kind.
