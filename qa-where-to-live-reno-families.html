Relocation · Families

Where should a family live when moving to Reno?

Four answers cover most families: South Reno and Damonte Ranch for newer homes and newer schools; Sparks and Spanish Springs for the most house per dollar; Somersett for foothill master-planned living with trails out the door; and the Carson Valley when 'yard' means acreage and the pace should drop by half. Which one is yours depends on commute, budget, and what your weekends look like.

Straight answers

Where should a family live when moving to Reno?

The usual shortlist: South Reno/Damonte Ranch (newer everything), Sparks/Spanish Springs (value and parks), Somersett (foothill master-planned), the Carson Valley (space and small-town pace). One conversation about commute and budget usually cuts it to two.

How do schools work for a relocating family?

Big county districts (Washoe around Reno-Sparks, Douglas in the Carson Valley) plus charters. Quality varies school by school — we connect relocating families with current local parents instead of quoting ranking sites.

Should we rent first with kids?

If the neighborhood is still a guess, rent near the top-choice school for six months. If you already know, buy once and move once. We'll tell you honestly which camp you're in.
