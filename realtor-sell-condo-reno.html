Selling a condo or townhome in Reno, NV

Condo sales succeed or stall on the association: buyers' lenders scrutinize HOA finances, owner-occupancy ratios, insurance, and litigation, and a sale can die on the building's paperwork rather than your unit's. NV Ball Real Estate Group sells condos with the association file assembled up front — resale package ordered early, lender-killers identified before they're surprises — and prices your unit inside the building's actual recent story, not the citywide average.

Package my condo sale

The association file, first

Resale documents, budgets, reserves, insurance certificates, litigation status, owner-occupancy ratio — ordered early, read carefully. Half of condo escrow drama is association paperwork arriving late and reading badly; we front-load it.

Lender reality

Some loan programs won't lend into buildings with certain litigation, insurance gaps, or investor-heavy ratios — which quietly shrinks your buyer pool. We identify which buyers can actually finance your building before pricing to them.

Pricing inside the building

Your best comps usually share your walls. We price against the building's and complex's recent sales, floor and view adjustments included — buyers shopping condos know the building's story; the price must too. Buying instead? The buy-side guide mirrors this page.

Straight answers

What do I need to sell my condo in Reno?

The usual prep plus the association file — resale package, budgets, reserves, insurance, litigation status — ordered EARLY. Lenders read it harder than buyers do, and sales die on building paperwork more than unit problems.

Why do condo sales fall apart more than house sales?

Financing — loan programs have building-level rules, and a building that fails them quietly shrinks your buyer pool to cash. Know your building's lender status before listing; skip the week-four surprise.

How should a condo be priced?

Against its own building first — the comps share your walls. Floor, view, condition adjust from there. Buyers know the complex's story; your price has to.
