Buying · Timelines

How long does it take to close on a house in Nevada?

For a financed purchase, plan on roughly 30 to 45 days from accepted offer to keys — the time underwriting, appraisal, and inspections genuinely take. Cash buyers can close in one to two weeks. The dates in your specific contract govern, and keeping them is a team sport: that's what our concierge transaction department exists for.

Straight answers

How long does it take to buy a house in Nevada after going under contract?

Typically about 30–45 days from accepted offer for a financed purchase; cash can close in one to two weeks. Your contract sets the real dates — our concierge department's job is making sure they hold.

What happens during those weeks?

Earnest money, inspections (and repair negotiation), appraisal, final underwriting, walkthrough, signing, recording. Nevada closes through escrow companies — we drive the timeline with them weekly.

Can I start house-hunting from California before my move date?

Yes — we write your move date into the offer's timeline. Many relocation clients tour by video and close right around their start date. See the relocation guide.
