Relocation · The long-distance signature

Closing on a Nevada home from out of state: how it works

You don't need to be in Nevada to close on a Nevada home: escrow companies run long-distance signings routinely — mail-away packages (documents overnighted to you, signed before a notary where you are, overnighted back), remote online notarization where the documents and parties qualify (Nevada authorizes RON; the title company confirms what your specific package allows), and powers of attorney for the narrow cases that need one (lender-approved in advance, never improvised). The buyer's coordination points: timing the signing days before recording (overnight shipping is part of the schedule), wire-fraud discipline mattering double at distance, and the final walkthrough delegated to us with live video when you can't fly in. Bay Area buyers close with us this way constantly — here's the machinery.

Coordinate my remote closing

The three signing paths

Mail-away (the workhorse: packages overnighted, signed before a local notary where you live, returned on a tracked label — build the shipping days into the timeline), remote online notarization where the lender, title company and document set allow it (Nevada authorizes RON; your specific package's eligibility is the title company's call to confirm early), and powers of attorney for narrow needs — lender-approved in advance, with the document drafted to their requirements.

The distance disciplines

Wire-fraud rules matter double remotely (verify wire instructions by phone at a known number, never from email — the full protocol), the signing appointment books ahead of the deadline rather than on it, and identification requirements get confirmed before the notary visit. Boring is the goal; early coordination is how.

What we handle on the ground

The final walkthrough on live video with our eyes on what the camera might miss, utility and key logistics timed to recording, and the sight-unseen playbook where the whole purchase ran remotely. You fly in to a finished closing — or just to the keys.

Straight answers

Can I close on a house in Nevada without being there?

Yes — mail-away signings, RON where your package qualifies (Nevada authorizes it; title confirms), or a lender-approved POA. Build in shipping days, verify wires by phone, delegate the walkthrough to live video.

What is a mail-away closing?

Escrow overnights the package; you sign before any local notary; it returns tracked — booked days ahead so shipping is schedule, not suspense.

Do I need a power of attorney to close remotely?

Usually not — mail-away and RON cover most cases. POAs are for narrow needs, lender-approved and drafted in advance, never improvised.
