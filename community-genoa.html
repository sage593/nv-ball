What living here is like

Genoa runs on charm earned honestly: a village core from 1851, cottonwoods over the main street, the Candy Dance filling the town each fall, and Jobs Peak standing watch over all of it. Around the historic center, the Sierra bench carries some of the Carson Valley's most coveted estates — view properties that pair the village's story with modern luxury. Life is quiet by design; the valley's services sit ten minutes east, and Tahoe waits over the grade.

Who Genoa fits

Strong fit: buyers who want history with their view; estate and luxury buyers drawn to the Sierra bench (this is Carson Valley's marquee address); Tahoe-adjacent living in sunshine; anyone for whom 'the oldest saloon in Nevada' is a neighborhood amenity.

Honest considerations: inventory is scarce and specific — patience is part of the purchase; services are village-scale, with errands in Minden/Gardnerville; estate pricing demands honest comparables, not romance; and foothill parcels carry the usual elevation homework (water, access, fire insurance).

Genoa FAQ

Is Genoa a good place to live?

If history and views top the list — there's nothing else like it in Nevada. The only catch is patience: Genoa inventory is scarce and specific by nature.

What is the Candy Dance?

The town's famous fall fair, running since 1919 — it funds the village and fills it completely for one sweet weekend a year.

Is Genoa a luxury market?

The Sierra bench above the village is the Carson Valley's marquee estate country — squarely our luxury division's territory.
