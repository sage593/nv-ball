What living here is like

West Reno runs out along the Truckee like the city exhaling: Mayberry Drive's corridor of parks, river path, and established homes; the quiet pockets around Dorostkar Park where cottonwoods do the landscaping; and Mogul — a compact riverside community with its own I-80 exit — holding the city's true western edge before Verdi. Life here is measured in river access: morning path miles, evening fishing light, and a drive to downtown short enough to feel like staying home. It's some of Reno's most settled, least-advertised living.

Who Mogul & West Reno fits

Strong fit: households who want the river as a daily amenity, not a weekend trip; path runners and cyclists (downtown is a ride, not a drive); buyers seeking established quiet with city services minutes away; and Tahoe-bound commuters who like I-80 at the corridor's spine.

Honest considerations: inventory is small-pocket and irregular — the right listing takes patience; river-adjacent living carries floodplain homework we run as standard diligence; freeway proximity means checking sound at specific addresses; and nightlife is a drive — this edge of town chose calm.

Mogul & West Reno FAQ

Is West Reno a good place to live?

For river-centered living, it's one of the city's best-kept answers — the Truckee as a daily amenity, downtown in ten. Honest notes: thin inventory, floodplain homework near the water, and sound checks near I-80 — we run both as standard.

What is Mogul?

Reno's true western edge: compact, riverside, own freeway exit — the last Reno address before Verdi, and priced like a secret it mostly still is.

How's the river path?

The corridor's quiet treasure: park-to-park miles toward downtown, morning runs, evening fishing light. For many residents it's the reason they bought.
