Relocation · The travel question

How is travel access from Reno? The airport, honestly

Reno-Tahoe International is a genuinely pleasant mid-size airport — minutes from most of the metro, security lines that coastal travelers find comical, nonstops to the major Western hubs and a rotating cast of further cities — and it is not a hub: much of the country connects through Denver, Phoenix, Salt Lake, or the California hubs, and international travel means connecting (or driving to SFO/SMF for direct long-haul). For most households this is a fine trade: ease at home, connections abroad. Heavy travelers should weigh it honestly — and plenty of them live here happily anyway. Here's the real picture.

Weigh my travel life

What the airport does well

Proximity (10-15 minutes from most of the metro), speed (parking-to-gate times big-city travelers don't believe), reliable nonstops to the Western hubs and majors, and seasonal ski-market routes that expand winters. For regional and hub-connected travel, it's honestly a luxury.

The honest limits

No hub status: much of the map means one connection; international means connecting or the SFO/SMF drive (Sacramento ~2 hours, SFO ~4) for direct long-haul. Routes rotate — specific cities come and go — so we speak in structure, not a route list that would go stale.

Planning around it

Travel-heavy households sort fast: hub-connected flyers (Denver/Phoenix/SLC patterns) barely notice; long-haul internationals build the connection or the occasional Bay Area drive into their rhythm. It's a real factor worth weighing before the move — our downsides page keeps it on the list on purpose.

Straight answers

Does Reno's airport have good flight connections?

Good for its size: Western-hub nonstops, rotating further routes, connections for much of the map, no direct long-haul international (connect, or drive to SMF/SFO). Compensation: ten-minute access and comic security lines.

Can I live in Reno with a heavy travel job?

Many do — hub-pattern flyers barely notice, and the airport's speed refunds time. The strain: frequent long-haul international. Weigh that one honestly before moving.

How far are the Bay Area airports from Reno?

SMF ~2 hours, SFO ~4 via I-80 — the occasional long-haul tool, not the routine. Most fly RNO and connect.
