Buying land to build on in Northern Nevada

A parcel's price tells you little; its feasibility tells you everything. Water (a producing well or a utility's will-serve letter), septic approval, legal access, utility reach, zoning, and fire-zone insurance — these decide whether a beautiful lot becomes a home or a lesson. NV Ball Real Estate Group guides land buyers with the feasibility checklist built into the offer, so you verify before you own.

Walk a parcel with us

The feasibility five

Water, waste, access, power, zoning — each verified in a due-diligence window written into your offer, with the right specialists brought in.

The paper matters

Easements, water rights, parcel maps, and county requirements. Rural paper is its own dialect; we translate.

Straight answers

Who helps buy land to build on in Northern Nevada?

We do — and with land, feasibility is the work: water, septic or sewer, access, easements, utilities, zoning, grading, fire-zone questions. The purchase contract is the easy part.

What makes a buildable lot actually buildable?

Water you can prove, waste you can permit, access that's legal, power you can reach, zoning that says yes — all verified inside a due-diligence window we write into the offer.

Where do people buy land around Reno?

Spanish Springs' north valley, the Verdi edges, the Carson Valley, the rural corridors east — each with its own water-and-zoning reality. The guides set the scene.
