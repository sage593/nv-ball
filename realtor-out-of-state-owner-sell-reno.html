Straight answers

Can I sell my Reno house if I live in another state?

Yes, and you never have to fly in. Video walkthroughs, local vendors wrangled, e-signatures, remote notarization (Nevada allows it). Many of our out-of-state sellers close without a single trip.

Who watches the house while it's listed?

We do. Utilities, landscaping, cleaning, security checks — with photos to your inbox after every vendor visit. Tended vacants sell; abandoned-looking ones invite lowballs.

What should out-of-state owners know about taxes?

The sale closes under Nevada rules, but your home state may tax the gain, and federal exclusions may apply if you once lived there. We flag the questions, your CPA answers them — and we coordinate 1031 timing when it fits.
