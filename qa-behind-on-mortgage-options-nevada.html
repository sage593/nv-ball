Behind on mortgage payments in Nevada: your actual options

Falling behind doesn't mean losing the house — and the worst move is silence. The real menu: reinstating or working out a modification with your lender, selling (many behind-on-payments owners have equity and don't realize it), or a lender-approved short sale if the home is worth less than the debt. NV Ball Real Estate Group walks these honestly — starting with whether a sale even makes sense for you — and flags the scams that target exactly this moment.

Talk through it confidentially

First: do you have equity?

Many owners behind on payments could sell, pay the loan, and keep real money — pressure hides the math. A confidential valuation is the first honest step; it costs nothing and it's often good news.

The lender-side options

Reinstatement, forbearance, and modification run through your lender or a HUD-approved housing counselor — legitimate, free help exists, and we'll point you to it rather than pretend a sale is the only answer.

Short sales, honestly

When the debt exceeds the value, a lender-approved short sale beats foreclosure for most owners' credit and peace — but it's a paperwork marathon needing lender consent. We've run them; we'll tell you if yours is viable before you commit.

Straight answers

What are my options if I'm behind on my mortgage in Nevada?

Four paths: restructure with the lender; sell (many behind-on-payment owners have equity and don't know it); a short sale if you owe more than it's worth; or foreclosure — which the first three exist to beat. Start with the equity math; it's free and often good news.

Will a short sale ruin my credit?

Less than foreclosure, usually, with faster recovery — but reporting varies. Counselor or attorney reads your file; we run the sale and don't play lawyer.

How do I avoid foreclosure-rescue scams?

Never pay up-front 'help' fees, never deed the house to a rescuer, distrust whoever materializes uninvited right now. Real counseling is free (HUD-approved); real agents get paid at closing, not before.
