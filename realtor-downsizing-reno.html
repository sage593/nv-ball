Downsizing · Reno & Northern Nevada

Downsizing in Reno? One plan, not two transactions.

Downsizing done well is choreography: the family home sold at its real value, the right smaller home found before the moving truck is an emergency, and the gap bridged — often with a rent-back that lets you move exactly once. NV Ball Real Estate Group coordinates both sides as one plan, with patience for the part no spreadsheet shows: leaving a home that raised a family.

Straight answers

Who helps with downsizing in Reno, NV?

We do — and we treat it as one plan, not two transactions: sell the family home, land the right smaller one, and bridge the gap with a rent-back when the timing needs it.

Where do Reno downsizers usually land?

Single-level newer builds in South Reno and Sparks, Del Webb at Sierra Canyon for 55+, low-maintenance townhomes, or the Carson Valley for smaller-house-bigger-view. We'll match the landing to the life.

What about decades of belongings?

We coordinate estate-sale, donation, and cleanout help, on a timeline that respects what thirty years in a home actually means. No two-week scrambles.
