Selling a house during a divorce in Reno

This sale has enough weight without a chaotic process. Our approach is built on three disciplines: strict neutrality (identical written updates to both parties and their attorneys), respect for legal timelines, and pricing that reflects the market rather than the moment's emotions. The goal is simple — a clean, well-documented sale that lets everyone move forward.

Straight answers

How does selling a house in divorce work in Nevada?

Once selling is decided — by agreement or court order — the mechanics are normal real estate with extra discipline: strict neutrality between parties, identical written updates to both, attorney timelines respected, proceeds through escrow per the decree. We've done this with care many times.

Can you work with both spouses?

Yes — neutrally, by design. Identical updates to both parties and their attorneys. Our client is the successful sale.

What if one spouse wants to keep the home?

Then the first need is an honest number — our market valuation is free — and a lender's read on the refinance. Your attorneys structure the rest; we supply the real-world figures.
