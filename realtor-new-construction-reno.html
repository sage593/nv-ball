New construction · Reno · Sparks · Beyond

Do I need a realtor to buy new construction in Reno?

You're not required to bring your own agent to a builder's sales office — but you should, because the model-home representative works for the builder. Your own REALTOR® negotiates incentives, upgrades, and contract terms, watches the build, and typically costs you nothing extra. The one catch: register your agent on your very first visit.

The first-visit rule

Builders honor representation registered at your first visit. Call us before you tour a model home — even a casual Sunday look can lock you out of representation.

Contracts aren't standard

Builder contracts are written by the builder's lawyers. We flag the clauses that matter: deadlines, deposit terms, and what happens if the build runs long.

Through the build
