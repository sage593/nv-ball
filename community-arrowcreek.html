What living here is like

ArrowCreek runs on range: the gate admits everything from polished semi-custom neighborhoods to estate parcels with the city glittering below, which makes it luxury's most versatile address in south Reno. The Club's two courses anchor the middle; trails climb from the back fences; and The Summit's conveniences sit ten minutes down the hill. Buyers who want gated foothill living with price flexibility usually end up here, comparing elevations.

Who ArrowCreek fits

Strong fit: luxury and move-up buyers wanting gated breadth rather than a single price point; view collectors (the city-light panoramas are the brand); golfers who like two-course variety; south-Reno professionals trading commute for elevation.

Honest considerations: the community's range means addresses vary meaningfully — elevation, view, and neighborhood each price differently (true-comparable work matters); wind and weather climb with the lots; club membership is its own decision with its own economics; and the gate adds minutes to every errand, which residents call a feature.

ArrowCreek FAQ

Is ArrowCreek a good community?

Yes — and its superpower is range: one gate, many tiers, from polished semi-custom to estate benches with the city glittering below.

ArrowCreek or Montrêux?

Montrêux is the benchmark; ArrowCreek is the range. Tour both — the comparison teaches buyers their own criteria faster than any description.
