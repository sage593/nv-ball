Straight answers

How do I sell land in Northern Nevada?

Not like a house. The buyer pool is thin, comps are scarce, and value lives in documentation: water, utilities, access, zoning, buildability. We price from that evidence, market to the builders and buyers who actually purchase land, and write feasibility terms that keep deals honest and alive.

Why is land pricing so hard?

No two parcels match — municipal water at the street versus well-and-septic future isn't a comp adjustment, it's a different asset. Pricing is an evidence case, which is why hopeful numbers sit unsold for years.

What documents help a land sale?

Everything that pre-answers feasibility: surveys, water-rights papers, well logs, septic or sewer status, utility will-serve letters, old plans or permits. Each answered question shortens escrow — we build the package before the sign goes up.
