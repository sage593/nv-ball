Relocation · The Nevada question

Should I move to Reno or Las Vegas?

They share a state and its tax advantages — no income tax either way — and differ in almost everything else. Reno: four seasons at 4,500 feet, the Sierra out the window, Tahoe at 45 minutes, a drivable 3.5–4.5 hours to the Bay Area. Las Vegas: big-metro scale, true desert heat, entertainment-capital energy, and SoCal within reach. Geography usually decides it — Bay Area households overwhelmingly land north, Southern Californians south — but climate and metro-size preferences deserve their vote.

Straight answers

Should I move to Reno or Las Vegas?

Same tax advantages, different lives: Reno is four seasons, the Sierra, Tahoe at 45 minutes, and a drivable run to the Bay. Vegas is big-metro energy, desert heat, and SoCal proximity. Bay Area movers usually pick Reno; SoCal movers usually pick Vegas — geography does most of the choosing.

How do the climates compare?

Reno has four real seasons and skiable mountains; Vegas has heat Reno never touches and winters that barely visit. Pick your weather honestly — you'll live in it.

Which is better for families leaving California?

Tiebreakers: which California family you're driving back to, your climate truth, and metro size preference. We're Northern Nevada specialists — and we'll say honestly if your list points south.
