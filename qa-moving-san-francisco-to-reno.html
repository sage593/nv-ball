Moving from San Francisco to Reno

The trade is explicit and the math is famous: housing dollars that stretch dramatically further, no state income tax, and Tahoe as the new backyard — against leaving a world-city's food, culture, and fog. What SF transplants discover: Reno's walkable pockets (Midtown, Old Southwest) deliver a surprising share of the life they liked, at prices that finally permit the rest of it. And the city they left stays four drivable hours away, which makes the leap feel — accurately — reversible.
