Relocation · The whole household

Moving to Nevada with pets and horses

Dogs and cats move easily — current vet records and attention to HOA pet rules cover most of it. Horses and livestock involve more: Nevada operates brand inspection requirements for horses entering or changing hands, transport health paperwork matters, and the real question is usually the property — turnout, water, fencing, and feed logistics vary enormously across our acreage communities. NV Ball Real Estate Group runs animal-first house hunts regularly: tell us the herd before the house, and we'll shortlist properties that actually work.

Plan the animal-first move

Dogs & cats: the easy part

Vet records current, microchips updated, and — the one that bites — HOA and rental pet rules checked before falling for a property. Some communities restrict counts, sizes, or breeds; we flag it during the search, not after.

Horses: the real checklist

Health certificates for transport, Nevada's brand-inspection framework for horses (a real thing newcomers don't expect — your hauler and the state's livestock officials handle the specifics), and a property vetted for water, fencing, turnout, and feed access. Palomino Valley and the Carson Valley are where we usually start.

The property is the plan

Acreage that pencils for animals is about systems: well capacity, irrigation or water rights, fencing condition, shelter, and winter access. We inspect all of it as standard practice — the animals' diligence list is longer than the house's.

Straight answers

What do I need to bring horses to Nevada?

Three things: transport health paperwork (vet and hauler know current rules), Nevada's brand-inspection framework (real, and newcomers never expect it — the state's livestock officials handle specifics), and a property that actually works: water, fencing, turnout, shelter. Paperwork's manageable; the property is the decision.

Are Reno-area HOAs strict about pets?

Varies widely — counts, sizes, breeds, and rentals add layers. The expensive mistake is checking after you're in love. We pull governing docs during the search, as standard.

Which communities work best for animal households?

Horses: Palomino Valley, Carson Valley, Washoe Valley, Pleasant Valley, Silver Knolls. Smaller menageries: most acreage communities, county rules varying. Tell us the roster; we map it honestly.
