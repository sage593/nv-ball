Relocation · Residency

How do I establish Nevada residency after moving from California?

Two things, in this order: actually make Nevada your home, then make every record agree. The practical list is short — Nevada driver's license, vehicle registration, voter registration, updated addresses everywhere that matters — but the test California applies is broader: where your life genuinely is. Here's the plain-English version our relocation clients start from.

Talk to an agent

The first-month checklist

Driver's license and vehicle registration at the Nevada DMV, voter registration, new address with banks, employers, the IRS, and insurance. Prompt beats perfect.

The bigger test

Domicile: your true home. Where your family sleeps, where your doctors and gym and mail are, where you return after trips. Make the honest answer 'Nevada' and the paperwork is easy.

Keep the receipts

Moving contracts, closing or lease dates, utility start dates, travel records. If a question ever comes, boring documentation ends it quickly.

Straight answers

How do I establish Nevada residency after moving from California?

Make Nevada your real home, then make the paperwork agree: driver's license, vehicle registration, voter registration, banks, doctors, and where you actually spend your nights. It's the whole picture that counts, not one document.

Is there a 183-day rule?

Days matter, but domicile — where your life genuinely is — matters more. Don't build the plan on a day count alone; have a CPA map your situation.

How soon should I start?

Before the move. The cleanest stories start on moving day — license, registrations, addresses switched promptly, records kept from the start.
