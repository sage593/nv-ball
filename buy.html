For buyers

Buying a home here, step by step.

Seven steps, no jargon. Your agent walks you through each one, and our concierge department tracks every date and document behind the scenes.

  1. 1 · Meet your agent

    A real conversation about what you want, what you can spend, and your timeline. No commitment yet — just clarity.

  2. 2 · Get pre-approved

    Before house shopping. Pre-approval (not just pre-qualification) means a lender has verified your finances, so sellers take your offer seriously. We'll connect you with trusted local lenders if you need one.

  3. 3 · House shopping

    Your agent curates showings against your wants-and-needs list, and tells you the truth about every home — including the ones to walk away from.

  4. 4 · Make an offer

    Price is one lever of many. We structure terms, timelines, and contingencies to win without overpaying.

  5. 5 · Under contract

    Escrow opens and our concierge team tracks every contingency and deadline so nothing slips.

  6. 6 · Inspection & appraisal

    We attend inspections, translate the reports, and negotiate repairs or credits when the findings justify it.

  7. 7 · Closing day

    Final walkthrough, signatures, keys. Then we stay your real estate resource long after the sale.
