Deciding · The fundamental fork

Should I rent or buy in Reno?

Skip the forecast; use the framework. Buying wins when three things align: you'll stay roughly five-plus years (transaction costs need runway to amortize), your income and upfront costs are genuinely handled, and you value control over flexibility. Renting wins shorter horizons, uncertain plans, and cities you haven't learned yet. Nevada's entry prices and tax treatment tilt the math toward buying sooner than coastal California's — but the five-year rule has survived every market we've watched.

Straight answers

Should I rent or buy in Reno right now?

Framework over forecast: buy when you'll stay ~5+ years, income is stable, and upfront costs are handled; rent for short horizons or a city you don't know yet. Your timeline outranks this month's rates — transaction costs need years to amortize either way.

Does Reno's rent-vs-buy math differ from California's?

Somewhat — friendlier entry prices and Nevada's tax sky tilt toward buying sooner than coastal CA's math. The five-year rule still rules.

What if I just moved here?

Then renting six months near your target area is often brilliant — one correct purchase beats one hasty one. Yes, we sell houses; we'll still say it.
