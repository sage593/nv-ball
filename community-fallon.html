What living here is like

Fallon runs on two engines that rarely sputter: the Navy base that brings the world's best aviators through town, and the irrigated valley that's fed Nevada for a century. Life is small-city genuine — Friday lights, farm stands, a courthouse square — with real ranch country beginning at the edge of town. Reno is an honest hour away, which residents treat less as a commute than as a periodic expedition.

Who Fallon fits

Strong fit: military families stationed at NAS Fallon (we work their timelines constantly); farm, ranch, and acreage buyers wanting the real thing; small-city people by temperament; value buyers for whom an hour from Reno is a feature, not a bug.

Honest considerations: Reno is genuinely an hour — daily commuting wears; services are small-city scale, with specialized medical runs to Reno; agricultural reality includes water-rights homework on farm parcels and the occasional feedlot breeze; and summer brings real heat to the valley floor.

Fallon FAQ

Is Fallon NV a good place to live?

For Navy families, ranch buyers, and small-city people — yes, sincerely. It trades metro amenities for genuine community and the greenest valley in the desert.

What is NAS Fallon?

The Navy's premier air-combat training base — and the town's steady heartbeat. Our military-move page covers the PCS side.

What should farm buyers check in the Lahontan Valley?

Water rights first — this valley runs on irrigation allocations — then wells, soils, and outbuildings. We bring the right specialists to the walk.
