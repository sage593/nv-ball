Selling an inherited house in Reno, NV

Selling a family home is a legal process and a human one. The legal part first: unless the home was held in a trust or with survivorship rights, Nevada probate may be required before anyone can sell — an estate attorney confirms your path. The rest is ours: honest valuation, as-is or prepared sale options, cleanout coordination, and patience with a family that's grieving while deciding.

Straight answers

How do I sell an inherited house in Reno, NV?

Step one is legal authority: unless the home was in a trust or held with survivorship rights, Nevada probate may be required — an estate attorney confirms which path applies. From there, we handle what we're licensed for: valuation, preparing the home, disclosures, and the sale, coordinating with your attorney throughout.

Do I have to clean out the house first?

No. As-is sales happen all the time, and we can coordinate estate-sale and cleanout help. Sometimes light prep pays for itself; sometimes it doesn't — we'll tell you which.

What is the house worth for the estate?

Our market evaluation is free. Note that estates sometimes also need a formal appraisal for tax purposes — your attorney or CPA will say whether yours does.
