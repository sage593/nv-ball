Relocation · The plan

Moving to Reno: a realistic 90-day timeline

The workable arc runs ninety days in three movements. Days 1–30: lender pre-approval, target-area homework (the community guides exist for exactly this), and video tours that shortlist honestly. Days 31–60: one well-built scouting visit, the offer, escrow opens. Days 61–90: closing, movers, and the same-week sweep of DMV, registrations, and addresses that makes Nevada residency clean. The whole thing compresses to 45 days when a job demands it — the sequence, not the speed, is what keeps moves from wobbling.

Straight answers

What's a realistic timeline for moving to Reno from out of state?

The 90-day arc: month one — pre-approval, area research, video tours; month two — scouting visit, offer, escrow; month three — close, move, DMV-and-addresses sweep. Compressible to 45 when needed; the sequence matters more than the speed.

What do movers wish they'd done sooner?

Three repeats: pre-approval before house-crushes, insurance quotes early, and the residency checklist on moving day, not at tax season.

Can the whole move run remotely?

Nearly — video tours, e-signatures, remote closing can shrink required presence to nothing. Most still fly in once, because neighborhoods are felt, not specced.
