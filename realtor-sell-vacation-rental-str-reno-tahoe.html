For STR owners · The exit

Selling a short-term rental in Reno or Lake Tahoe

Selling an STR is three sales in one: the property, the business (bookings, reviews, revenue history), and sometimes the furnishings — and the buyer pool splits between investors who want the operation and residents who want the house. Permit rules differ by jurisdiction and often don't transfer automatically, which shapes who can buy. NV Ball Real Estate Group sells STRs with the operating file packaged, the permit question answered up front, and both buyer pools courted on purpose.

The operating file

Revenue history, occupancy, reviews, and expense reality — assembled honestly. Investor buyers pay for evidence, discount for mystery, and walk from fiction.

The permit question first

STR rules vary by city and county around Reno-Tahoe, and permits often don't transfer automatically. We verify the current rules for your address before listing, because they decide which buyer pool is real.

Furnishings & bookings

Turn-key-with-furniture widens the investor appeal; future bookings need a handoff plan (or wind-down) written into the contract. Both are negotiation assets when handled early — the buy-side STR guide is this page's mirror.

Straight answers

How do I sell my Airbnb or vacation rental in Reno-Tahoe?

As a property plus a business: revenue history, occupancy, reviews, expenses — and the permit answer first, since it often doesn't transfer and decides your real buyer pool. Evidence sells; mystery discounts.

Do STR permits transfer to the buyer?

Depends entirely on the jurisdiction — transfer, reapply, or capped waitlist. We verify your exact address's current rule before listing; a general answer here would be wrong for someone.

Should I sell furnished with future bookings in place?

For investor buyers, often yes — turn-key commands attention, with bookings handed off by contract. Residential buyers want neither. Which pool you're really selling to answers the question.
