House-hacking a duplex in Reno, NV

The oldest good idea in real estate still works: buy a duplex (or tri/fourplex), live in one unit, let the rents carry the mortgage. Owner-occupied financing — low-down FHA and VA included — applies to 2-4 unit properties, and the rental income helps you qualify. Reno's small-multifamily inventory is thin, central, and competitive; NV Ball Real Estate Group builds house-hackers into ready buyers before the right one lists.
