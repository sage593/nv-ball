Relocation · The offer letter move

Moving to Reno for a TRIC or Gigafactory job: the playbook

Congratulations on the offer — now solve the geography: the Tahoe-Reno Industrial Center sits east of Sparks down USA Parkway, which makes the east side your friend (Spanish Springs, Sparks, D'Andrea) and Dayton the value play via the back route, while west-side addresses pay a cross-town tax every shift. The honest sequencing advice for most new arrivals: secure housing fast but buy deliberately — a strategic first lease beats a rushed purchase, and the purchase beats the second lease. Solve my commute first

The commute map

East Sparks and Spanish Springs: the standard answer, commonly a half-hour run. D'Andrea: the quiet winner. Dayton: the value route via Six Mile Canyon. South Reno: doable but taxed. We quote real shift-hour drive times, not map optimism — the fuller map is here.

Timing the start date

Tight start dates favor a fast lease in the right geography, then a deliberate purchase inside the year — the market rewards buyers who've learned their commute and their preferences. We set the search up during the lease, not after it.

Shift-work realities

Swing and night schedules change the calculus: commute direction flips, neighborhood quiet matters differently, and schedule-friendly showings are how we work anyway. Tell us the actual schedule; the map adjusts.

Straight answers

Where should I live if I work at TRIC or the Gigafactory?

East side: Spanish Springs and east Sparks standard (~half-hour), D'Andrea the quiet winner, Dayton the value route. West side pays a daily tax. We quote your actual shift-hour drive times first.

Should I rent or buy when I arrive for the job?

Usually: strategic lease first, deliberate purchase inside the year — you buy better after learning your commute and your preferences. Exceptions exist; we'll tell you honestly if you're one.

Does the TRIC commute have a winter problem?

USA Parkway: maintained, generally fine. The Dayton canyon route: more storm respect. Proper tires + forecast awareness covers most of it — we brief the unglamorous specifics.
