Relocation · The practical question

What is healthcare like in Reno for relocators?

Reno is Northern Nevada's medical hub: Renown Health anchors the region (including its major regional medical center), alongside Saint Mary's, Northern Nevada Health System, the VA's Sierra Nevada health care system, and Carson Tahoe Health serving the capital corridor. For most movers, access is solid and distances beat big-metro traffic math. The honest notes: some specialties run thinner than Bay Area depth, waits for certain specialists are real, and movers with complex ongoing care should map their specific needs before — not after — the move.

The lay of the land

Renown's network anchors Reno; Saint Mary's adds a second full system; the VA serves veterans from its Reno campus; Carson Tahoe covers the capital and valley. Most daily care sits minutes away, not freeway-hours.

The honest comparison

Against the Bay Area's university-medicine depth, some specialties here run thinner, and certain specialist waits are real. For complex or rare conditions, movers sometimes keep a California specialist and fly or drive — a plan worth making deliberately.

Where this meets housing

Proximity to care shapes neighborhood choice more than most movers expect — especially for retirees and anyone managing a condition. Tell us it matters; we'll weight the map accordingly.

Straight answers

Is healthcare good in Reno?

For its size — yes: multiple full systems, a VA campus, Carson Tahoe down the corridor. Caveats: thinner than coastal university medicine in some specialties, real waits in a few. Complex care gets planned before the move, not after.

Which hospitals serve the region?

Renown (the anchor), Saint Mary's, Northern Nevada Health System, the VA for veterans, Carson Tahoe for the capital corridor — with Tahoe and rural towns leaning on smaller facilities plus these hubs.

I have ongoing specialist care — what should I do before moving?

Three calls first: your specialist (records + referral), the Reno-side practice (REAL appointment availability), your insurer (Nevada network reality). A week of calls saves a year of scramble.
