What living here is like

South Meadows is where Reno's growth decades consolidated: the Double R Boulevard arc of office parks, Renown's South Meadows medical campus, and the planned neighborhoods that filled in around them — nineties-through-2010s homes, townhome clusters, and apartments threaded with parks and wetland greenbelts. The pitch is friction-free geometry: jobs, medicine, the Summit's retail, and I-580's spine all inside five minutes, Tahoe runs up Mt. Rose Highway from the doorstep, and the polish of master-planning without Damonte's newer-build premiums. It's the south side's engine room — and a perfectly pleasant place to live in it.

Who South Meadows & Double R fits

Strong fit: corridor workers — the live-five-minutes-from-work crowd at the offices and Renown South Meadows; households wanting south-side polish at nineties-to-2000s pricing rather than new-build premiums; townhome and low-maintenance buyers (the corridor's cluster stock is the city's deepest); and Mt. Rose skiers playing the highway's geometry.

Honest considerations: the corridor is commercial-adjacent by design — office-park rhythms, retail traffic, and I-580's hum vary block by block (we walk specific addresses); wetland-adjacent sections carry mapping homework we run as standard; HOA layers are nearly universal and vary widely in fee and temper; and buyers wanting estate quiet or acreage should climb the hill — this is polished density, chosen on purpose.

South Meadows & Double R FAQ

Is South Meadows a good area of Reno?

The south side's geometry answer: jobs, medicine, retail, and the freeway inside five minutes, at pricing under Damonte's new-build premium. Terms: commercial-adjacent rhythms (block by block), HOAs nearly universal, polished density by design.

South Meadows, Damonte, or Double Diamond — what's the difference?

Three generations of one idea: Double Diamond (90s, gentlest entry), South Meadows (live-work corridor), Damonte (newest, most polished, priced for it). Budget + vintage preference sort it fast.

What's the commute logic from South Meadows?

In-arc workers: minutes. Downtown: fifteen via 580. Tahoe/ski: Mt. Rose Highway from the doorstep. The exception: TRIC — an honest cross-valley haul better served from the east.
