Selling a rental property in Reno, NV

Selling a rental is a sequencing problem before it's a marketing problem: tenant-occupied or vacant, lease timing, Nevada's notice rules for showings and move-outs, and — if a 1031 exchange is in play — deadlines that shape everything upstream. NV Ball Real Estate Group maps the path with you first, then lists with the plan already settled. 93 homes sold in the last twelve months, investor exits among them.

The occupancy decision

Straight answers

How do I sell a rental property in Reno with tenants in it?

Three paths: sell occupied to an investor (the lease rides along), time the sale to lease expiration, or negotiate an early move-out. Nevada's notice rules apply throughout, and a 1031 plan changes the sequencing — so we map the path before the sign goes up.

Is it better to sell vacant or tenant-occupied?

Occupied keeps income and targets investors; vacant shows better and opens the bigger owner-occupant pool. Genuinely case-by-case — we'll run your version, not a rule of thumb.

What about taxes when selling a rental?

A real tax event — recapture, gains, and possibly a 1031 deferral with its strict clocks. We coordinate with your CPA from day one; we don't play one.
