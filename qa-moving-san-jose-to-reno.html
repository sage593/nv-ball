Moving from San Jose & Silicon Valley to Reno

The Valley-to-Reno move runs on an equity engine: a Silicon Valley home converts into Reno's best neighborhoods with meaningful money left over, hybrid work survives the move (physical-presence tax rules, CPA-blessed), and the outdoor life Valley weekends chased becomes the Tuesday default. Tech transplants skew toward South Reno, Somersett, and the Galena corridor — newer construction, trails out the door, and an airport that keeps badge-in weeks painless.
