Relocation · Business owners

Can I move my business to Nevada?

Commonly, yes — and owners do it steadily: Nevada levies no state corporate or personal income tax and runs on well-established entity law, and the mechanics range from forming a new Nevada entity to redomesticating your existing one. The honest complexity sits in substance: where the work is actually performed, where customers sit, and California's continuing reach over operations that remain there. Paper domicile without operational reality invites the exact scrutiny people hoped to escape. This is attorney-and-CPA territory; we handle the real-estate side of the same move, with tested introductions to the entity-side professionals our business-owner clients used.
