The listing agreement: what you're actually signing in Nevada

The listing agreement is a contract hiring your agent, and its handful of decisions deserve two minutes each: the term (how long you're committed — and what happens if the relationship sours), the compensation (negotiable, stated plainly, including what's offered toward buyer-side representation), the cancellation terms (ours lets unhappy sellers leave — ask any agent for theirs in writing), the protected-buyer clause (buyers introduced during the listing, should you cancel and sell to them after), and the service promises worth writing down rather than trusting to memory. NV Ball Real Estate Group walks every clause before any signature — here's the map so you can walk it with anyone.

Walk the clauses with me

Term and exit

Listing terms commonly run months, and the real question is the exit: what releases you if service disappoints? We offer a plain cancellation policy because confident service doesn't need a lock — and any agent's answer to 'how do I leave?' tells you plenty before you sign.

Compensation, plainly

Commission is negotiable — always has been, explicitly so post-settlement — and the agreement states your total, including what's offered toward the buyer's side (a marketing decision we make together, segment by segment). Judge it against the net and the service, in writing, before signing.

The quiet clauses

Protected-buyer periods (reasonable in principle — know the length and the list), dual-agency consent you can decline (our explainer), dispute-resolution terms, and the service list — marketing, communication cadence, the machine itself — written, not vibed.

Straight answers

What should I look for in a listing agreement?

Five clauses: term + exit rights ('how do I leave?' is diagnostic), total compensation incl. buyer-side offer, protected-buyer length and list, dual-agency consent (declinable), and services in writing. Good agents walk them gladly.

Can I cancel a listing agreement in Nevada?

Per its terms — read the cancellation clause BEFORE signing. Ours releases unhappy sellers; get any agent's answer in writing during the courtship.

Is the commission in the agreement negotiable?

Yes — explicitly so now. Evaluate fee against service and projected NET; the cheapest fee that nets less is the expensive one.
