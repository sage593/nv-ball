What living here is like

Midtown is the Reno you walk: coffee, dinner, a show, and the river path without touching the car. The housing stock is the charm and the homework — bungalows and brick cottages with real character, real mature trees, and the occasional real knob-and-tube wiring question. It's the neighborhood for people who'd rather have a Tuesday-night scene than a three-car garage.

Who Midtown Reno fits

Strong fit: walkability-first buyers; creatives, academics, and downtown or hospital workers; anyone allergic to HOA life; buyers who light up at 'original hardwood floors.'

Honest considerations: inventory is older, smaller, and competitive — inspections matter and so does moving fast; garages and big yards are scarce; some blocks sit closer to nightlife noise than others (we know which); and renovation budgets deserve respect here.

Midtown Reno FAQ

Is Midtown Reno a good place to live?

If you want to walk to dinner and love homes with a past — yes, it's the best square mile in Reno for exactly that. Just respect the inspection and the renovation budget.

Are the old homes a problem?

Not a problem — homework. We inspect for the usual 1940s suspects, and priced right these bungalows are the most loved homes in the city.

Is it loud?

Depends on the block — the spine has Friday opinions, two streets over is crickets. We know which is which.
