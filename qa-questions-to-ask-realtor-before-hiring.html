Questions to ask a realtor before you hire one

Interviewing agents is normal, smart, and rarer than it should be — most people hire the first friendly voice — so here's the actual interview, with what good answers sound like: production ('how many homes did you or your team close in the last twelve months?' — verifiable recent volume beats decades of license age; ours is 93), systems ('what happens to an inquiry on my listing at 8 PM?' — the honest answer names software and minutes, not vibes; ours is Follow Up Boss inside five), marketing ('show me the last three listings' actual media and reach'), pricing honesty ('tell me about a listing you priced wrong and what you did'), and references that are recent, not curated classics. Interview two or three; any professional worth hiring respects the process. Here's the full question set — including the ones to ask us.

Interview us first

The production questions

'How many closings in the last twelve months — you personally, and your team?' (recent, verifiable volume; vague seniority is not an answer). 'How many in my price range and area?' (a luxury specialist learns your first-time market on your dime, and vice versa). 'Team or solo — and who exactly will I be working with day to day?' (both models work; surprise handoffs don't).

The systems and marketing questions

'What happens to a buyer inquiry at 8 PM Saturday?' (names and minutes, not 'I'm very responsive'). 'Show me actual media from your last three listings.' 'Where does my listing get marketed beyond the MLS, specifically?' 'How will you communicate with me, how often, and in writing?' The answers separate operators from marketers in about four minutes.

The honesty tests

'Tell me about a listing you overpriced — what happened and what did you do?' (everyone has one; watch whether you get a story or a dodge). 'When would you tell me NOT to sell or buy?' 'What's your fee, and what exactly does it buy?' And check the reviews yourself — recency and specifics over volume. Our twenty consecutive 5-star reviews are public; read them like a skeptic.

Straight answers

What should I ask a realtor before hiring them?

Ask: recent closings (you and team), what happens to an 8 PM inquiry (software and minutes, not vibes), the last three listings' real media, a mispriced-listing story, and fees in plain words. Interview 2–3; pros respect it.

How do I know if a realtor is good?

Recent verifiable production, visible systems, recent specific reviews, and honest answers to 'tell me one you got wrong.' Operators and marketers diverge by question four.

Is it rude to interview multiple real estate agents?

No — it's the professional norm. Any agent worth hiring respects being compared; wariness about it is itself an answer. Two or three, same questions, decision makes itself.
