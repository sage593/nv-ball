What living here is like

The University District runs the blocks around UNR where Reno's oldest housing stock earns its keep: early-1900s cottages and bungalows, small multifamily tucked mid-block, and a rental market with campus-calendar depth. It's two neighborhoods at once — investors read cap-rate logic and perpetual tenant demand; owner-occupants and first-timers read walkable-to-campus-and-downtown character at entry pricing with sweat-equity upside. Both readings are correct, which is why the district's listings move to whoever understood the block first.

Who University District fits

Strong fit: investors who want tenant-demand depth and small-multifamily opportunity (the house-hacking classic); first-time buyers trading polish for location, character, and entry pricing; university households who want the commute to be a sidewalk; and renovators who can read century-old bones honestly.

Honest considerations: century-old stock means century-grade diligence — foundations, plumbing, knob-and-tube eras — and we inspect accordingly; the campus calendar sets the soundtrack on some blocks (game days, semester rhythms) — walk your block at night before offering; parking is street-Tetris in the core; and block-by-block variance is the district's whole truth — fifty yards changes everything.

University District FAQ

Is the University District a good investment?

The logic is real — perpetual campus demand, small-multifamily stock, entry pricing. The discipline: read block by block, inspect century-old bones, walk it at night. It rewards homework and punishes spreadsheet-only buying.

Can first-time buyers still get in near UNR?

Yes — one of central Reno's real entry points: character cottages below Midtown pricing, sweat-equity upside. Trades: block variance, campus soundtrack, old-house upkeep. For the right buyer, walkable Reno's best deal.

What should I check before buying near campus?

The century (foundation, plumbing, electrical eras), the block (Friday night AND Tuesday morning), and the rules (zoning, rentals) if investing. We run all three, standard.
