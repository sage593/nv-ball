Who is a good realtor on the Nevada side of Lake Tahoe?

We represent both sides of the lakeside equation: the alpine property itself, and the Nevada-residency reasons many buyers choose this shore. Our luxury division covers the premium segment.

Why buy on the Nevada side of Tahoe rather than California's?

No state income tax, a friendlier property-tax structure, and for some buyers the home anchors a genuine Nevada residency. Plus the east shore's quieter character — which suits some buyers and not others, and we'll say which you are.

What should Tahoe buyers know before offering?

Snow loads, winter access, STR rules that change by jurisdiction, wildfire-zone insurance. Alpine homework, all manageable — if it's in the plan before the offer.
