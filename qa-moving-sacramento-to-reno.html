Relocation · The two-hour move

Moving from Sacramento to Reno: what to know

Sacramento to Reno is the gentlest California-to-Nevada move on the map: about two hours up I-80, close enough for weekend trips back, far enough for no state income tax and a different pace. You already know valley heat and wildfire smoke; you'll trade Delta breezes for Sierra snow and a smaller, mountain-backed metro. NV Ball Real Estate Group has guided the full spectrum of California movers — Sacramento's version is the easiest to get right.

What actually changes

No state income tax, four-season weather with real snow, a metro you can cross in twenty minutes, and Tahoe from the other side — closer to the Nevada shore's calm than the California shore's crowds.

What stays familiar

Valley summers, smoke-season awareness, I-80 as your main street, and family two hours away. This move keeps your California life reachable — the relocation guide maps the rest.

The hybrid play

Two hours door to door makes a short office week workable, and structuring remote work properly decides how much of the tax benefit you actually keep.

Straight answers

Is moving from Sacramento to Reno worth it?

Often, yes — it's the gentlest California exit there is: two hours up I-80, ties intact, no state income tax, four real seasons. Honest trade-offs: snowier winters, smaller metro, and wildfire smoke you already know from Sacramento summers.

Can I keep my Sacramento job and live in Reno?

Hybrid workers make it work — two hours door to door suits a one-or-two-day office week, and fully remote workers gain the most. California still taxes California work days, so structure it properly; our remote-work guide covers how.

How does housing compare between Sacramento and Reno?

Closer than the Bay Area comparisons — neighboring mid-size metros, not different planets. Reno's dollar buys newer master-planned supply, mountains out the window, and Nevada's tax treatment. Live numbers in conversation; published ones go stale.
