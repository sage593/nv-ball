Moving from Sacramento to Reno: is it worth it?

This is the gentlest version of leaving California: roughly two hours up I-80, which keeps parents, clients, and season tickets within a day trip; Tahoe sitting in the middle like a shared backyard; and Nevada's no-income-tax sky waiting on the far side.
