Smoke season in Reno: the practical guide nobody wrote

The honest pages mention it; this one handles it: some summers deliver smoke weeks — regional fires (often hundreds of miles away) sending haze that grays skies and moves outdoor plans indoors — and the local adaptation is a learnable kit, not a resignation: AQI literacy (the apps every local runs, the thresholds that change plans), the home's defenses (HVAC filtration upgraded, the portable-purifier rooms, the close-the-house rhythm), the plan flexibility (smoke days are the winter-storm days of summer — indoor alternatives staged), and honest variance — some summers pass nearly clean, others test the kit. Movers deserve the real brief; residents deserve the playbook. Here's both.

Build the smoke kit

The AQI-literate household

The apps locals actually run (AirNow and peers), the thresholds that move plans (sensitive groups' lines versus everyone's), and the pattern literacy — smoke arrives on forecasts like weather, varies block-to-valley less than people assume, and clears on wind shifts worth watching. A week of attention builds the fluency.

The home's defenses

HVAC filters upgraded for the season (higher-rated filters sized to the system), portable HEPA purifiers staking out bedrooms and the main living space, the close-up rhythm (windows shut ahead of arrival beats chasing smoke already inside), and newer builds' tightness earning its keep. The maintenance calendar carries the filter line.

The honest variance

Some summers pass nearly clean; others gray out real stretches — the variance is the truth, and the downsides page keeps it on the ledger on purpose. What residents report: the kit plus flexibility metabolizes it the way winter metabolizes snow — a season managed, not a verdict endured.

Straight answers

How bad is wildfire smoke season in Reno?

Variable: some summers nearly clean, others bring gray weeks from distant fires. The kit: AQI fluency, upgraded filtration + purifiers, close-house rhythm, plan flexibility. A season with a playbook.

What should I buy to prepare for smoke season?

Modest: higher-rated HVAC filters, 1–2 HEPA purifiers (bedrooms first), AQI apps, the close-up rhythm. A few hundred dollars versus experiencing it as siege.

Does smoke season affect which house I should buy?

Lightly but really: tight newer builds filter readily; swamp coolers pull outside air by design; interface zones add the shutoff layer. A diligence line, not a search-reorganizer.
