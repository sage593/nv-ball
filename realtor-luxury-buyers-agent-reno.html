Luxury buyer's agent in Reno, NV

Buying at the top of the Reno–Tahoe market is a different craft from selling there: the best properties often trade quietly, pricing has fewer comparables and more opinion, and the diligence — water, views, HOAs, club obligations — runs deeper. Straight answers

Do I need a buyer's agent for a luxury home in Reno?

More, not less, than elsewhere: top properties trade quietly, pricing is argument rather than lookup, and the diligence list is long. The job is access, evidence, and discretion — that's what you're hiring.

How do you find homes that aren't listed?

Networks, owner outreach in your target enclaves, and the expired-and-withdrawn file of would-sell owners. Unglamorous persistence — which is where the luxury market actually lives.

Which areas does your luxury practice cover?

The whole circuit: the gated corridor (Montrêux, ArrowCreek, Rancharrah, Galena), the view benches, Tahoe's Nevada shore Incline-to-Glenbrook, and Genoa's estate country.
