How much do you really need for a down payment?

The question we hear more than any other — and the answer surprises most people: you almost certainly don't need 20% down. Twenty percent avoids mortgage insurance, but it is not the price of entry. Here's what the main loan types actually require:

Loan typeMinimum downMortgage insuranceMinimum credit score
Conventional3%On down payments under 20%620
FHA3.5%For 11 years or the life of the loan500
VA (veterans & spouses)NoneNone620
USDA (designated rural areas)NoneNone640

Two more things buyers often don't know. First, pre-qualified and pre-approved are not the same thing — pre-approval means a lender verified your documents, and it's what makes sellers take your offer seriously. Second, down payment assistance programs exist in Nevada, and lower-credit buyers are often closer to qualifying than they assume.

The right move is a fifteen-minute conversation with a lender before you fall in love with a house. We'll happily introduce you to the local lenders our clients trust.
