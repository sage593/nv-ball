Selling · Timing

When is the best time of year to sell a house in Reno?

The conventional answer is spring through early summer — the buyer pool is deepest and the yard looks its best. The more useful answer: 'best' is a function of competition as much as demand, and a well-presented home in thin-inventory November can command more attention than one lost in a crowded May. What the calendar can't tell you — this month's inventory on streets like yours — is exactly what we can.

Straight answers

When is the best time of year to sell a house in Reno?

Conventionally spring to early summer — deepest buyer pool, best curb appeal. But thin winter inventory can mean more attention per serious buyer. Your street's current competition matters more than the calendar, and that's a live read we give free.

Does winter kill a Reno sale?

No — relocations don't wait for June, and winter listings face less competition. Light it well, warm it up, clear the walk, and winter sells.

Should I wait for the market to improve?

You usually buy in the same market you sell in, so 'waiting for better' often nets to zero. The better question is whether the move makes sense — and we'll be straight with you about it.
