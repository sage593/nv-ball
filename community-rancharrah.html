What living here is like

Rancharrah is what happened when Reno's most storied private estate — casino pioneer Bill Harrah's ranch — became a neighborhood: roughly 141 acres of gated village where new luxury homes and villas sit along the old estate's bones, an equestrian center keeps the ranch soul, and The Club at Rancharrah anchors the social calendar with pool, spa, fitness, and dining. The location is the quiet trick: this is estate-feeling living ten minutes from Midtown, the airport, and south Reno's commercial spine, with the Marketplace's restaurants at the gate.

Who Rancharrah fits

Strong fit: buyers who want luxury without acreage chores — lock-and-leave villas to full-scale homes inside one gate; club-centered social lives; equestrians who want their horses minutes, not hours, away; professionals who priced Montrêux's forest and chose the city's middle instead.

Honest considerations: this is HOA-and-club living with the fees that fund it — budget for both; lots are village-scale, not ranch-scale, so acreage seekers belong in Galena or the Carson Valley; construction has rolled through in phases, so ask what's still building near any lot you love; and resale inventory is thin — patience or a build are the usual paths in.

Rancharrah FAQ

What is Rancharrah?

Bill Harrah's legendary ranch, reborn as Reno's most central luxury enclave — gated homes and villas, an equestrian center, a private club, and the Marketplace's restaurants at the gate.

Is Rancharrah a good investment?

Its position is rare: central, gated, club-anchored, finite. Live numbers in conversation — published ones go stale — but scarcity and location do the durable work here.

Do you have to join The Club at Rancharrah?

Terms have varied by phase and product, so we verify the current membership and HOA obligations for the exact home you're considering — part of standard diligence, not an afterthought.
