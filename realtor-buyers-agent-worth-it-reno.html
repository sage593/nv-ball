Straight answers

Do I need a buyer's agent in Reno — and who pays them now?

Not required — and today it starts with a written agreement stating exactly what we do and how we're paid, which is negotiable and often still handled through the transaction via negotiated seller concessions. What changed with the settlement rules?

Mechanics more than economics: written agreements first, compensation explicitly negotiated. Transparency favors buyers who ask questions — we're glad to be asked.

Can I just use the listing agent?

You can — against a professional whose duty runs to the seller. Some unrepresented buyers do fine; most leave money or protections behind. Your call, with eyes open.
