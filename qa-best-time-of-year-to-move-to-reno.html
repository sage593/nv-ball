Relocation · The calendar question

What's the best time of year to move to Reno?

The honest answer: the season matters less than movers fear, and each has a real case. Late spring through early fall moves easiest (passes clear, daylight long, kids between school years) and competes hardest for homes and movers; winter moves are cheaper and less contested but gamble with Sierra storms if your truck crosses Donner; shoulder seasons — our quiet favorite — split the differences. And the meta-truth: the right time is when your life says go, with the season shaping logistics rather than vetoing them. NV Ball Real Estate Group lands movers in every month — here's the season-by-season field guide.

Time my move sensibly

The seasonal cases

Summer: easiest logistics, hardest competition, school-calendar alignment. Winter: gentler competition and mover pricing, storm-window planning for the I-80 crossing, and you meet your new home honestly (see it in January, love it anyway). Shoulders: mild everything — the connoisseur's answer.

The storm-window reality

Winter crossings of the Sierra are planned, not feared: storms arrive in forecastable cycles, Donner's chain controls and occasional closures pass in hours-to-a-day, and flexible truck scheduling beats white-knuckle bravado. The winter checklist picks up from arrival day.

The market's seasons, honestly

Listing inventory swells spring-summer and thins winter — more choice versus less competition, a genuine trade with no universal winner. We time house-hunting to your move's reality rather than folklore; the rent-first sequencing question matters more than the month.

Straight answers

What's the best season to move to Reno?

All four have cases: summer = easy logistics, hard competition; winter = cheap and calm with storm-window planning; shoulders = the connoisseur's split. Move when life says go; season shapes logistics, not the decision.

Is moving over Donner Pass in winter a bad idea?

A planned idea: forecastable storm cycles, closures that pass in hours-to-a-day, flexibility beating bravado. Thousands cross weekly; the stories come from ignoring forecasts.

Is it better to house-hunt in winter or summer here?

Summer: more choice, more rivals. Winter: thinner pickings, gentler negotiating, honest-January showings — an underrated diligence trick. Your timeline decides.
