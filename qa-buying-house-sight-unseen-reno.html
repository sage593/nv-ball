Relocation · The remote purchase

Buying a house in Reno sight unseen: how to do it safely

Relocating buyers close on Reno homes they've never walked through regularly — done right, it's a process, not a gamble: live video tours shot the unflattering way (we show you the road noise, the slope, the neighbor's project car), independent inspections read deeply, neighborhood drive-bys at different hours on your behalf, and contract terms that keep exits open until your evidence is in. NV Ball Real Estate Group runs remote purchases as a discipline; the buyers who get burned are the ones whose agent pointed the camera only at the granite.

Set up my remote purchase

Video, the honest way

Live walkthroughs where you steer; footage of the street, the slope, the sound, the sightlines — not just the staging. We send the unflattering angles on purpose; surprises after closing cost more than honesty before.

Verification stack

Full inspections read line by line with you on video; specialist inspections where the property type demands; HOA documents and permit history pulled; neighborhood passes at morning, rush hour, and night. Your evidence file substitutes for your feet.

Terms that protect distance

Inspection and appraisal contingencies kept intact, remote notarization arranged, and a final pre-closing video walkthrough before funds move. Remote buying fails when buyers waive protections to compete; we compete on certainty instead.

Straight answers

Can I buy a house in Reno without seeing it in person?

Yes, regularly and safely — as a discipline: honest video (unflattering parts included), inspections reviewed together, neighborhood passes at different hours, documents pulled, contingencies intact. The risk isn't distance; it's a camera pointed only at the granite.

What should a remote buyer insist on?

Live video YOU steer, street and sound included; inspections read together line by line; a filmed pre-closing walkthrough; contingencies intact until each box checks. An agent who resists that list is the wrong agent.

Should I fly out at all?

Ideally once — for the shortlist, not the search. One trip validates the finalist. Can't come? The discipline closes safely without it; plenty of ours have.
