Luxury FAQ

Do you work with out-of-state luxury buyers?

Constantly — it's a specialty. Video tours, scouting itineraries, and introductions to lenders and CPAs who know CA-to-NV moves. Many clients buy here before they've fully relocated.

What does a luxury listing consultation involve?

A private walkthrough, a candid pricing conversation against current comparables, and a written marketing plan. No obligation, and discretion is assumed from the first call.

What price range counts as luxury in Reno?

The threshold moves with the market, which is why we don't print a number here — in conversation we'll show you exactly where the luxury segment starts this season and how your home or budget sits within it.
