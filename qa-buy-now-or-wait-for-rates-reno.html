For buyers · The timing question

Should I buy now or wait for rates to drop in Reno?

Honest answer up front: nobody — not us, not your lender, not the confident voice online — knows where rates go next. What's knowable: your monthly payment at today's numbers, whether that payment fits your life, and the fact that if rates fall meaningfully, refinancing exists while waiting buyers flood back into competition. The decision isn't a rate forecast; it's whether the right house at an affordable payment exists for you now. NV Ball Real Estate Group will tell you plainly if it doesn't.

Pressure-test my timing

The refinance asymmetry

Buy at today's rate and rates fall: you refinance. Wait and rates fall: you compete with everyone who waited, often on price. The asymmetry isn't subtle — but it only matters if the payment works today, which is the real test.

What waiting actually buys

Sometimes: savings, clarity, a better position. Waiting is right when the payment doesn't fit, the down payment needs time, or your life situation is genuinely unsettled. We'll say so — a stretched buyer helps no one, including us.

Structures that bridge

Seller-funded rate buydowns and credits are live in this market and can cut the early-years payment meaningfully. The deal structure, not the forecast, is where a buyer has actual control.

Straight answers

Should I wait for mortgage rates to drop before buying?

Nobody knows rates — so drop the forecast. Payment work today on a house you'd be happy in? Buying-then-maybe-refinancing beats waiting into the returning crowd. Payment doesn't work? Then waiting isn't market timing; it's just true.

What happens to Reno prices if rates fall?

Magnitudes unknowable; mechanism known: cheaper money expands budgets and returns sidelined buyers — upward price pressure. That's the quiet cost of waiting nobody prices in.

Is it a buyer's market in Reno right now?

It shifts monthly and by segment — anything printed here goes stale. Live answer in conversation, with the data open in front of us.
