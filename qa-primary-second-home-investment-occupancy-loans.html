For buyers · The three checkboxes

Primary, second home, or investment: why the occupancy checkbox matters

The checkbox prices your loan: primary residences get the best rates and lowest down payments (you live there — lenders' favorite risk), second homes pay modestly more under genuine-personal-use rules, and investment properties pay the most with the largest down payments (rents can help you qualify, though). The temptation this creates — checking 'primary' or 'second home' for a property you'll rent — has a name, occupancy fraud, and it's the misrepresentation lenders verify hardest, with consequences that outlast the savings. The honest menu is workable at every tier; NV Ball Real Estate Group makes sure the plan and the paperwork match — here's the map.

Match my plan to the box

The three tiers

Primary: best pricing, lowest down, occupancy expected within the move-in window and maintained genuinely. Second home: modest premium, genuine personal use, typically distance-and-usage rules, rental limits by program. Investment: the honest rental tier — higher rates and down payments, but rental income helps qualification and every strategy is on the table.

Why fudging fails

Lenders verify: occupancy checks, utility and address records, insurance types, and data-matching that's only gotten sharper. Discovered occupancy fraud can mean loan acceleration, denied insurance claims (your landlord policy doesn't exist), and worse. The savings are real; the risk is the whole house. The honest tier always pencils better than that.

Planning the honest version

Buying to house-hack? Owner-occupied multifamily is the legitimate best-of-both (the duplex playbook). Tahoe escape that rents sometimes? Second-home rules have real room — planned deliberately. Pure rental? Investment financing with rents qualifying — the investor lanes run fine at honest pricing.

Straight answers

What's the difference between primary, second home, and investment property loans?

Pricing follows risk: primary (best terms, real occupancy), second home (modest premium, genuine personal use), investment (highest pricing, rents help qualify). The box must match the plan — occupancy fraud is the most-verified lie in lending.

Can I rent out a house I bought as my primary residence?

After genuinely living there (commitments typically run about a year) — yes, conversions are normal and whole strategies depend on them. Planning immediate rental behind a 'primary' checkbox is the fraud version.

How does a second home differ from an investment property at Tahoe?

Genuine use decides: second home = real personal use, limited rental per program; investment = the rental business, priced as such. STR plans usually mean investment financing — have the honest lender talk first.
