Relocation · Transplant questions

Does Nevada have Mello-Roos?

No — Mello-Roos is California's invention and stays there. Nevada's nearest cousins are SIDs and LIDs: Special and Local Improvement District assessments attached to some newer developments, funding roads and infrastructure through a line on the property-tax bill for a set period. They're not universal, they're fully visible in escrow and county records, and the remaining balance and term are exactly the kind of thing we hand buyers in plain numbers before an offer — because California transplants have been trained to ask, and here the answer is checkable.

What SIDs fund

Roads, utilities, infrastructure in newer communities — amortized across the benefiting parcels for a defined term. Transparent, finite, checkable.

Where they appear

Some newer developments, not all — community-specific. Routine due diligence on our newer-construction purchases includes the check. New-build buyers, note.

The negotiation angle

Straight answers

Does Nevada have Mello-Roos?

No — that's a California invention. Nevada's cousins are SIDs/LIDs: improvement-district assessments on some newer developments, riding the tax bill for a set period. Not universal, fully disclosed in escrow, and worth reading — we flag them on every affected property.

How do I know if a home has a SID or LID?

Title/escrow documents and county records show it. We check as routine on newer communities and hand you balance and term in plain numbers.

Can SIDs be paid off early?

Often — and sometimes it becomes a negotiation chip in the sale. Payoff versus assumption is a terms-and-plans question we price for you.
