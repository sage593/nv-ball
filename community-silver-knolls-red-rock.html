What living here is like

North of Reno's last subdivisions, the valley floor opens into acreage country: Silver Knolls' established one-acre-and-up parcels with mountain backdrops, and the Red Rock corridor running US-395 north through high-desert spreads toward the state line. Life here is the acreage covenant — horses, shops, stars, wind — with a practical twist the remoter rural areas can't match: the North Valleys' big-box services sit ten to fifteen minutes away, and downtown Reno under half an hour. It's the region's most reachable version of genuinely rural living.

Who Silver Knolls & Red Rock fits

Strong fit: horse and hobby-farm households wanting acreage inside a real Reno commute; shop-builders and toy-haulers (the parcels and codes accommodate); dark-sky and elbow-room buyers priced out of closer-in acreage; and value-hunters who understand wells and septic.

Honest considerations: wind is the honest headline — the northern valleys blow, regularly and with conviction; wells, septic, and propane are standard homework we run parcel by parcel; winter here runs colder and whiter than the city floor; services beyond the North Valleys' basics mean a Reno run; and parts of the broader area carry flood-and-playa history worth checking by address.

Silver Knolls & Red Rock FAQ

Is Silver Knolls a good place to live?

For rural-without-isolation, one of the region's most practical answers: acre-plus parcels, mountain backdrops, services inside fifteen minutes. Terms: the wind means it, wells-and-septic homework, colder winters than town.

What should I check before buying acreage here?

The full rural file: well production, water rights, septic, propane, road maintenance status (some are private), site-specific wind exposure, and area flood/playa history. Standard checklist here — we run all of it.

Why is this area's pricing gentler than acreage closer to town?

Distance, wind, and owning your own systems — honestly priced in. Which is exactly the opportunity if max-land-per-dollar inside a Reno commute is the brief.
