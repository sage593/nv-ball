Buying · Fire country

Is wildfire insurance hard to get around Reno and Tahoe?

The honest answer is property-by-property: valley-floor Reno and Sparks homes generally insure routinely, while foothill, forest-edge, Verdi, Galena, and Tahoe properties can meet higher premiums, selective carriers, and mitigation requirements. The variable you control is timing — insurance quotes belong in the first days of escrow, inside your contingency window, where a hard number is information instead of a crisis. That's how we run every fire-adjacent purchase.

Straight answers

Is wildfire insurance hard to get in Reno and Tahoe?

Property-by-property: valley floors insure routinely; foothill, forest-edge, and Tahoe homes can face higher premiums and pickier carriers, with mitigation increasingly moving both. Our practical rule is non-negotiable: quotes in escrow's first days, never its last.

What helps a home insure better in fire country?
