How to research schools when moving to Reno

The structure: most of the region sits in the Washoe County School District (Reno-Sparks), with Carson City, Douglas County (Carson Valley), and Lyon County (Dayton, Fernley) running their own districts, plus charter and private options. What we won't do is rank schools — fair-housing law and honest practice both say families weigh schools themselves. What we do well: explain attendance zoning, flag that boundaries change, and point you at primary sources — the districts' own data — so your shortlist rests on your judgment of real information.

The map, structurally

Washoe County SD covers Reno-Sparks; Carson City, Douglas, and Lyon counties run their own; charters and privates layer on top. A home's assigned schools are address-specific and boundaries get redrawn — we verify assignment during escrow, never assume it.

Research that actually works

Districts publish real data; state report cards add more; touring beats both. Online ratings compress a school into a number — families who visit consistently report the number missed what mattered, in both directions.

How we help without ranking

We'll never tell you a school is 'good' — law and honesty agree that's your call. We will verify zoning for any address, time tours into your house-hunting trips, and structure offers so a family's timeline survives escrow. The family-areas guide pairs with this page.

Straight answers

How do I research schools when moving to Reno?

Go primary: district data (Washoe County SD for Reno-Sparks; Carson City, Douglas, Lyon for the valleys), the state's report cards, and tours — which catch what numbers miss. Verify a home's actual assignment (boundaries move); treat portal ratings as a starting point, not a verdict.

Why won't you just tell me the best schools?

Law and honesty, together: steering by school characterization violates fair housing, and 'best' is genuinely yours — fit, size, philosophy. We verify zoning and point at primary sources; the judgment stays yours.

Can I count on a home's school assignment staying put?

Mostly — not absolutely. Growth redraws boundaries and opens schools. We verify current assignment in escrow and flag known rezoning talk; a guarantee doesn't exist, and honest agents say so.
