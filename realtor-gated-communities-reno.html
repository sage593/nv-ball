Straight answers

What are the gated communities in Reno, NV?

The principal names: Montrêux and parts of Galena Forest on the Mt. Rose corridor, ArrowCreek in the south foothills, gated enclaves within Somersett and Caughlin Ranch — plus Clear Creek and Genoa's bench estates south in the Carson Valley. Each gate guards a different life; the guides below tell them apart.

What does a gate actually buy?

Privacy and controlled access, paid in dues, guest logistics, and rules. Half our buyers find that trade perfect; half find it daily friction. Decide which you are before falling for a house.

Which gated community is 'best'?

They're different answers: Montrêux the benchmark, ArrowCreek the range, Somersett the amenities, Galena the pines. Your criteria pick the gate.
