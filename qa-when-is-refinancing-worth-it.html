For owners · The math, not the forecast

When is refinancing actually worth it? The framework

The honest framework needs no rate prediction: a refinance is worth it when its benefit outruns its cost within your realistic horizon — closing costs divided by monthly savings gives the break-even month, and owning past it is the whole game. The traps are equally timeless: resetting a seasoned loan's clock (five years into thirty, a 'lower payment' that restarts amortization can cost six figures of long-run interest — run the term-matched comparison), rolling costs into balances and calling it free, and refinancing for cash-out reasons that deserve the HELOC comparison instead. NV Ball Real Estate Group isn't a lender — but owners ask us constantly, so here's the arithmetic that doesn't go stale.

Run my break-even honestly

The break-even spine

Total refinance costs ÷ true monthly savings = break-even months; your realistic ownership horizon beyond that = the win zone. Under it, the refinance pays the lender twice. The arithmetic takes five minutes and survives every rate environment — which is why frameworks beat forecasts.

The term-reset trap

The screen shows a lower payment; the amortization shows a restarted clock: five years into a thirty, refinancing into a fresh thirty trades real long-run interest for monthly optics. The honest comparison matches terms (a 25-year quote against your remaining 25) — lenders quote it when asked, and the ask is the discipline.

The occasions that genuinely justify

Meaningful rate improvement surviving break-even math, removing mortgage insurance a better LTV no longer needs, exiting adjustable terms ahead of resets, buyout-driven restructures, and the delayed-financing round trip. Rate-chasing without the math is the lender's favorite customer; don't be the favorite.

Straight answers

How do I know if refinancing is worth it?

Break-even first: costs ÷ monthly savings = the month it starts paying you; your horizon decides. Then match terms — a fresh thirty against your seasoned loan hides a restarted clock. Five minutes; no forecast needed.

Is it bad to restart a 30-year mortgage when refinancing?

Expensive when hidden: fresh thirties restart the interest-heavy years. Honest versions: match your remaining term, or take the thirty and pay the old schedule. Ask for both quotes.

Should I refinance to pull cash out?

Compare first: cash-out reprices your WHOLE loan; old-low-rate + HELOC usually wins that math. And the cash's purpose deserves its own honesty check.
