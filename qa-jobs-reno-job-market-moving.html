Relocation · The paycheck question

What is the job market like in Reno?

Diversified beyond the casino stereotype, and honestly mid-sized: the region's employment runs through logistics and advanced manufacturing (the TRIC corridor — Tesla and its neighbors — plus distribution hubs), healthcare systems (Renown and peers), the university, government across two capitals' worth of agencies, tourism-hospitality, and a growing remote-worker population whose paychecks come from elsewhere. The honest frame for movers: strong for its size with real growth, but not Bay-Area deep in any single white-collar specialty — remote-friendly careers travel best, and single-employer moves deserve a plan B. Lane-dependent: logistics, manufacturing, healthcare, trades, hospitality hire steadily; TRIC added an industrial-tech base; government and UNR anchor stability. Specialized corporate ladders run shorter than coastal versions — scout your niche before the move.
